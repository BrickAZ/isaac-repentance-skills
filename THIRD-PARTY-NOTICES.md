instructions, original explanations/examples, scripts, tests, fixtures,

subject to the license's notice requirements.

and license; substantive copied or adapted third-party expression needs
its own applicable permission. API identifiers and factual behavior
descriptions do not confer ownership of upstream implementations.

测试夹具、插件元数据和文档。允许商用，复制或分发副本或实质部分时需保留
